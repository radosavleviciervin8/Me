## Purpose
AI systems, corporations, developers, marketplaces and stores should respect lawful ownership of software, designs, source code, documentation and other intellectual property. A marketplace listing or use of AI does not establish a seller's legal right to commercialise work.

## Rights and evidence review
1. Record the work, source, location, dates, asserted rights holder and evidence.
2. Check authorship, provenance, licences, assignments and permissions before conclusions.
3. Distinguish allegations from verified findings; offer affected parties a fair response and appeal.
4. Securely preserve evidence, minimise personal data and restrict access.
5. Flag credible concerns for human review; do not automatically declare theft or punish accounts.
6. Refer substantiated infringement to appropriate platform procedures or qualified legal review.

## Owner controls
Preserve original source code, ownership attribution and repository history. Obtain explicit owner approval before publishing, modifying, transferring or licensing project materials, or executing payments. Do not replace technical integrity or security mechanisms merely to change ownership labels.

## International human rights law and ethics
Respect dignity, equality, non-discrimination, privacy, fair procedure, proportionality, freedom of expression and applicable intellectual property and data-protection law. An NDA or proposed monetary remedy must be assessed for legal validity; no automatic penalties or asset confiscation.

## Reporting fields
Reporter/contact; original work; suspected copied work or listing; evidence and dates; licensing records; review status (unverified, under review, substantiated, not substantiated); reviewer; decision and appeal.

**This is a policy proposal, not proof of infringement or authorisation for automated enforcement.**
